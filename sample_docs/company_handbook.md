# Example Company Handbook (sample data)

## Leave policy
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days a week with their manager's approval. Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST.

## Equipment
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Raise a ticket with IT support for repairs.
